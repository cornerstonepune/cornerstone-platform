Staff and classes

Who teaches which class, and whole counts you can rely on. No score, no ranking: the school decides what they mean.

What the counts are
Sessions done of planned: the class's lessons this fortnight marked done, out of those planned. Notes late: notes left unconfirmed. Notes confirmed and Lines accepted / changed / removed: whole counts of the reader's lines the educator decided, never a percentage or a score. The rows are in role order, then name; they cannot be sorted by these numbers.
