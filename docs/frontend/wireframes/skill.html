Easy
Counts the bundles of ten, with a prompt.
Medium
Counts in tens up to 50 with bundles.
Hard
Counts in tens up to 100 and adds loose ones.
Advance
Says how many without recounting.
Kinds of question
  • Count the bundles
  • Fill in the missing ten
  • Match the number to the bundles
Mistakes it watches for
  • Counts each stick by ones, not in bundles.
  • Says the number before the ten.
