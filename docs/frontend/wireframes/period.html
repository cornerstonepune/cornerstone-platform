Counting in tens with bundles

K2 Red

What it is for

Counts in tens up to 100 with bundles.

The steps

  1. Give each pair a handful of sticks and some rubber bands.
  2. Bundle ten sticks. Say "one ten".
  3. Count the bundles in tens, then add loose sticks.

Lay out

Bundling sticks
200
Rubber bands
30

Watch for

A child who counts each stick by ones instead of bundling. Ask: "How many tens do you have?"
