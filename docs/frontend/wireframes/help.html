Each page

Today Today door
What it is for
What waits on you today: periods to teach, notes to confirm, papers to check.
What you see where
Your periods in time order at the top; the notes waiting for you under them, oldest first.
What each button does
A period opens it. A note's Confirm opens the marks the reader drafted; nothing is ticked for you.
Record a note from a period
What it is for
Saying what happened in a period, by Sr. No., in your own words.
What you see where
The period and activity at the top; Start recording under them.
What each button does
Start recording, Stop, Send. Add a photo of the work. Type the marks if you cannot speak.
Class picture Children door
What it is for
How every child in a class is doing on one skill.
What you see where
A square per child with its state word; groups with counts under it.
What each button does
A square opens that child's recent history. Open the child goes to their page.
Timetable Timetable door
What it is for
The fortnight, week A and week B, each period a block.
What you see where
A moved period has an amber outline; a clash is red until the coordinator accepts it.
What each button does
A cell opens its period. A moved cell's details say who moved it and why.
Reports Children door
What it is for
Each child's report, line by line, in your words.
What you see where
Rows grouped by stage, with counts; a child's lines in a fold.
What each button does
Approve (educator) on each of your lines. Preview opens the parent's copy.
