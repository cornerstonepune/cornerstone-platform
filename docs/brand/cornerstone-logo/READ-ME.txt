CORNERSTONE GURUKUL - LOGO FILES

Which file to use
- lockup-colour          Mark + "Cornerstone गुरुकुल" in dark ink. For white or light backgrounds.
- lockup-for-dark-backgrounds   Same lockup with the mark on a white tile and light text. For dark or photo backgrounds.
- mark                   The courtyard mark alone (small spaces, social profile, stamp).
- mark-on-white-tile     The mark on a rounded white tile (app icon, dark backgrounds).

Formats
- SVG / PDF: vector, any size, never blurry. Use these for print, standees, signage, flex boards. Text is already converted to shapes, so no fonts are needed.
- PNG: transparent background, 3x size (lockup 4500 px wide). Use for Word, PowerPoint, WhatsApp, web.

Colours (fixed)
- Courtyard walls: terracotta #B8562F
- Dot and dark text: basalt #26241F
- Hindi on dark: clay #F3C9B3

Rules
- Never recolour the mark. No maroon, no black-only, no white-only version.
- On a dark ground the mark always sits on the white tile (use the dark-background file).
- Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the dot-to-wall square (about a quarter of the mark's height).
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or put it on a busy photo without the white tile.
- Fonts: Young Serif (Cornerstone), Eczar Bold (गुरुकुल).
