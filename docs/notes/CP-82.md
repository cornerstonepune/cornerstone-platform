# CP-82: stock and the buy list

## Engine (branch wt/stock)

What was built, and where:

- `supabase/migrations/20270212090000_stock.sql`: the ledger `stock_move` (kinds in, out, count, writeoff, minimum; append-only through
  `internal.forbid_change`, so the engine and the website roles lose update and delete; a count keeps what the books said in `expected`)
  and the view `stock_level` (the last count plus the moves after it, the newest minimum). Table budget 86 to 87, `raised_by` names CP-82.
- Settings (`supabase/seed/config.json`): `stock.count_tolerance` (`units` and `percent`: a count within the larger of the two needs no reason),
  `stock.reasons` (the words a count and a write-off offer), `stock.access` (`keeper: ops`, `every_campus: coordinator`, roles on the money ladder).
- `core/stock.py` (access, the five moves, on hand, history), `core/stock_words.py` (the plain words), `w2_print/stock_buy.py` (the buy list,
  on step N4 of `workflows.json`, built from `materials_consolidate.gather`, now public with `total`, cut to the campus's classes).
- Routes `api/routes/stock.py`, all POST with `actor` in the body as Money's are: `/stock/access`, `/on-hand`, `/in`, `/out`, `/count`,
  `/writeoff`, `/minimum`, `/history`, `/buy-list`. `apps/web/lib/engine-routes.ts` and `engine/checks/rules.json` regenerated.
- Tests: `tests/test_stock.py` (Done-when 1 to 7 through the functions) and `tests/api/test_stock_routes.py` (shapes, refusals, no-store refused).

Decisions a reader should know:

- A first count of an item the books have no move for needs no reason (nothing to compare it with); after that, a difference above
  `max(units, percent of the books)` does, and a refused line refuses the whole count (nothing saved).
- Buy list: `to_buy = max(0, need + minimum - max(on_hand, 0))`; an unknown need gives `need: null, to_buy: null` (to confirm), never a smaller
  number. A store whose books went below zero (`count it`) is counted as holding none, so wrong books never inflate a purchase.
  Items with no need but under their minimum are on the list (need 0). `classes_without_list` names the campus's classes that have a plan
  for the month but no materials list yet, whose need is therefore not in the lines.
- A hand-out may go to any class of the school (the campuses lend to each other), but the store, the claim and the keeper are the campus's.
- The core list (`tests/core.txt`) is at its budget of 230, so none of these tests joined it; two candidates are the append-only and the
  who-sees-Stock tests in `tests/test_stock.py`.
