# CP-86: record what arrived, from the claim that bought it

## What was built (branch wt/arrived, on the main working branch with Money, Stock and the picker)

Engine, no new table and no migration (`stock_move.expense_id` already names the claim):

- `core/stock_arrived.py`: `arrived(conn, staff, claim)` and `arrive(conn, staff, claim, lines)`. A line is the ordinary `stock.stock_in` naming the claim, so the ledger,
  the on-hand and the history say it already. `arrive` runs the lines in a savepoint (`conn.transaction()`), collects every wrong line and refuses the whole call with
  "Nothing was saved." and a sentence per line (the item is named), so the good lines of a refused call are undone.
- `core/stock.py`: `keeps()` (the campuses a person keeps, or a refusal) split out of `keeper()`; `core/money_claims.py`: `standing_of()`, the claim's campus and standing
  as My claims says it (public, so Stock does not reach into `_claim`).
- Routes (`api/routes/stock.py`, POST, `actor` in the body): `/stock/arrived {actor, id}` answers `{claim: {id, campus, status}, can_record, moves: [{on, item,
  material_code, quantity, unit, by}]}` (stock ins that name the claim, newest first); `/stock/arrive {actor, id, lines: [{material_code, quantity}]}` answers
  `{recorded: n}`. Refused in words: a person who keeps no store (403, asked first, so a stranger learns nothing of the claim), a keeper who does not act for the claim's
  campus (403, "That claim was made for X; you keep the store of Y."), a claim that is not approved or paid (422), a claim that is none (422), a line with no quantity above
  zero or an item not in the catalogue (422, all lines named). `can_record` is true for a keeper of the campus on an approved or paid claim.
  `apps/web/lib/engine-routes.ts` regenerated; `checks/rules.json` did not change (no migration).
- Tests: `tests/test_stock_arrived.py` (Done-when 1 to 4 through the functions), one route test in `tests/api/test_stock_routes.py` (shapes, refusals).

Screens (`apps/web`):

- The claim's panel, in My claims (`money/claim-panel.tsx`) and To pay (`money/claim-queue.tsx`, asked by `pay/page.tsx`): for a keeper, on an approved or paid claim,
  a quiet secondary button **Record what arrived** among the claim's actions (it reads **Record more** once something arrived), and an **Arrived** tab beside Details and Bill.
  Nobody who keeps no store gets either: the page asks `lib/stock-arrived.ts` `arrivedOf`, which asks the frame's own (cached) `/stock/access` first and never calls the
  engine for them, nor for a claim that is not approved or paid.
- The form (`money/arrived-form.tsx`, a `<details>` in the panel like Stock's item forms; no new page): a row is the one item picker and a quantity; **Add another item**,
  **Remove this item**, one **Save**. A row left empty is not sent; a quantity with no item is told "Choose the item that came." in the web (as the stock-in form does). The
  engine's refusal stands beside the line it names (`lib/arrived-words.ts` `byLine`, read from the item's name in its sentence) and what was typed stays. Saving closes the form,
  says "Recorded: n items." and opens the Arrived tab (`?tab=arrived`), which lists each item with its quantity, unit, the day and who recorded it. Server action
  `money/arrived-actions.ts` (one `/stock/arrive` call; revalidates Stock).
- Guide: `guide/pages/stock.json` (a job and a button) and `money.json` (a line about the right side).
- Browser spec `tests/u126-arrived.spec.ts` (six tests at 1440, the layout one also at 400 px; none `@core`). The browser core stays at 120 of 120 (no page route was added, u90 not touched).
- `u121-money-approve-pay.spec.ts`: a founder keeps every store, so the To pay panel now holds this form too and `getByLabel("How")` matched its "How many"; the two lookups
  of the payment mode use the select's name instead.

  several rows each searching the catalogue on focus was not measured.

- Looked at by eye at 1440 and 400 px: the button, the form with two items, a refused line, the Arrived list, To pay with the list. Not looked at: a screen reader, the keyboard
  path through two pickers, a keeper of two campuses.

- Matching the claim's amount to what arrived, reading the bill and a claim that arrives in parts (CP-86 out of scope) are not done.
- Two lines of one item in one call are two moves (the picker stops it in the screen, the engine allows it).
- The engine tests ran on a database a browser spec had not yet written to: Playwright inserts campuses (`U126 Annexe …`), and `tests/test_stock.py::test_only_a_keeper_sees_stock…` then
  fails on that database because a coordinator keeps every campus. It passes on a fresh database; CI runs the engine and the browser on separate ones.
