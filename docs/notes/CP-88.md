## What was built

- `ClassGrid`: a cell with `selected` set is a toggle (`aria-pressed`); when on, a tick takes the place of the initials and the accent ring
  (a shadow, so the dashed "not seen" outline and the focus ring are left alone) goes round the square.
- `ChildLevels`: a tap picks or lets go (the old `ticked` set); the one-child picker, `choose`, `close` and the checkbox row are gone;
  "Select all unmarked" sits above the grid beside the hint; "Level for the rest" is as it was. One bar (`data-block="LevelPicker"`,
  `data-level-picker="ticked"`) with the count, Clear and every level and mark; a choice calls the same `save({ rolls, l })`. Escape clears.
  `saveLessonLevels`, the as-expected defaults and the history are untouched.
- The bar is `sticky` under the grid on a desktop and `fixed` above the 56 px tab bar on a phone: a sticky bar cannot rise above the top of
  its section, so a child tapped low on the screen left it half under the tab bar (seen in a screenshot). The section is a flex column, not a
  grid, for the same reason on a desktop (a grid item may not leave its cell).
