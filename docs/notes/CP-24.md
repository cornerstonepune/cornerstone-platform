## Added after the verifier's first FAIL (9 Nov, at 28d9e846)
- **Every school date now comes from the school's setting.** The engine reads `settings.school_today` / `school_day` /
  `time_zone` (and `settings.ZONE_SQL` inside SQL); the website reads `lib/school-clock.ts` or the `school.time_zone` row. Fixed
  this round: the voice routes' default day, who may record, the coordinator and class-picture grants, the people import's
  start day and the grant it writes, the nightly route's fallback key, the two daily-spend counts (`adapters/llm.py`,
  `w3_read/external.py`), days since a question was given (`edit_paper`), the parent report's week, `live_data.week_now`, and
  the across-classes page's "days missing" query.
- **A new ordinary test** (`tests/test_one_clock.py`) fails if engine, `apps/web/lib` or `apps/web/app` source holds
  `date.today(`, a zone-less `datetime.now()` or `current_date`.
- **Left on purpose, not school dates:** `voice_intake.receive` takes the storage key's year from `datetime.now(dt.UTC)` (a
  folder name), the voice clean-up route's idempotency key is the UTC hour (`datetime.now(dt.UTC)`), `nightly.py` and
  `checks/live.py` stamp explicit-UTC moments, and `timetable.py` / `timetable_rules.py` already ask `voice_intake.school_day`.
- **`apps/web/lib/week.ts` `isoWeek()` now takes the school's day** (done after the first check, at the orchestrator's word): `schoolWeek()`
  in `lib/school-clock.ts` gives the school's current week, the pages, the print route and the queries use it, and
  `tests/u72-the-week-is-the-schools.spec.ts` shows 01:00 on a Monday in Pune is that Monday's week.
- **The plan routes have no role check.** `/fortnight/move`, `/timetable/link` with `apply` and `/timetable/generate` check only that
  the actor is on the staff list. `/timetable/link` records the constant `engine timetable link` as who linked, not the actor. The website layer must
  check the coordinator group before calling them (line added to the checklist in `docs/notes/CP-4.md`). No role check is built in this scope.
