# CP-85: materials that are alternatives ("pencils or crayons")

## What was built, and where (branch wt/alt, on the Stock, catalogue and picker work)

- `supabase/migrations/20270215090000_activity_material_alternatives.sql`: ONE nullable column, `activity_material.alt_group` (integer, 1 or more). Items of one
  activity with the same number are alternatives; null is needed on its own, which is every row there is today. No new table (table budget unchanged); `checks/rules.json`
  regenerated from a clean migrations-only database and gained one rule, the column's check (`activity_material_alt_group_check`).
- The class list (`w2_print/materials_list.py`, with the pure `w2_print/materials_alt.py`): the build makes ONE line for each lesson's group, the first alternative by
  `ord` or the one the educator swapped to, and writes the others, name and code, on each lesson's entry of the line's `sources` (`alternatives: [{code, name}]`). `read`
  answers `alternatives` on each line: only the alternatives EVERY lesson of the line names (a lesson that needs the item on its own, or names another pair, leaves none, so
  taking the item away never leaves a lesson without it).
- `POST /materials/list/swap {actor, line_id, material_code}` (`api/routes/materials.py`, `w2_print/materials_swap.py`): the educator who holds the class (the add and remove
  routes' own check). The line becomes the alternative; the choice is a `change_log` row of op `swap` (before and after `{material_code}`, who), which is what the next build reads
  to keep it (a swapped line stays the group's line, and a line of that item the educator swapped to is never made twice). The list is built again, so the line's quantity and unit are the
  swapped item's own. Answers `{line_id, material_code, item, alternatives}`. Refusals: 403 not hers or screens off, 404 no such line, 409 the line was removed or the list already has a line for
  that item (taken off or not: `unique (list_id, material_code)`), 422 not one of its alternatives.
- The school's list (`materials_consolidate`) and the buy list (`stock_buy`) read class lines, so they count the one line per group with no change; proved by a test, not assumed.
- The splitter (`w1_bank/alt_groups.py`, by code, no new prompt): in the apply step (`map_ledger._material`), two items the split returned are one group when the school's note
  has each once and only "or" or "and/or" (and at most a number of the second) between them, in any case; a chain "A or B or C" is one group. Left as before: an item the note
  names twice, a pair with an "and" on its outer side ("glue, paper and pencils or crayons" can be read two ways), a word between ("or a duster"). A line the existing checks refuse
  still goes to a person, unchanged; a group left with one item (its partner is not an item) is dropped (`forget_lonely`).
- The activity page's "Edit the materials" (`w1_bank/k2_review.set_materials`): the upsert never touched `alt_group`, so a person's edit of other lines keeps it; the text written
  from the list now says "A × 10 or B × 12" for neighbours of one group; an item left alone in a group is cleared. On the page (`components/activity-page.tsx`) a quiet "or"
  takes the icon's place before the second alternative. No new editor: a person cannot yet CREATE or break a group by hand, only keep it (see below).
- Screens: the class list (`app/(app)/timetable/materials/page.tsx`) says "or crayons" after the item (muted, small), and the line's panel (`line-panel.tsx`) has one quiet text button
  "Swap to crayons" for each alternative (`swapLine` in `app/(app)/educator/materials/actions.ts`); a previewer sees the "or" and no button. `lib/engine-routes.ts` regenerated.
- Tests: `tests/test_materials_alt.py` (Done-when 1 to 4 through the functions, and the detector's table), `tests/api/test_materials_alt_routes.py` (the swap route), `tests/u125-alternatives.spec.ts`
  (browser, at 1440 and 400 px, none `@core`; the browser core stays at 120 of 120, `u90` unchanged). Nothing joined `tests/core.txt`.

## Decisions a reader should know

- A group is per activity (the same number on the rows of one activity); "alternatives across activities" and "the cheapest alternative" are out of scope, as the spec says.
- The build resolves a lesson's group to one item: the one she swapped to (a line of this list with a `swap` change whose item is in the group), else the first by `ord`. A swap applies to the
  whole line, so the offered alternatives are only those every lesson of the line names.
- The swapped line keeps `source = 'school'` (it still comes from the plan); that she chose is the `swap` row of `change_log`, like a quantity she set.
- The quantity of a swapped line is the swapped item's own from the plan, unless she set a quantity (then hers stays, as it always did).

## Not verified

- Nothing ran against live or a copy of live. The real K2 notes ("Puppets or flashcards") are not in this repository: the seed json holds only the G1 to G4 activities' materials (853 texts). Run over
  those with a stand-in for the model's split (the text cut at commas, semicolons, "or" and "and", a leading number and filler dropped), the detector finds 68 groups in 60 of the 67 texts that hold the
  word "or"; it was never run over the model's real split or over the K2 notes, so how many real notes get a group is an estimate.
- The split's names reach the detector as the model gave them; a name the model rewrites ("pencil" for "pencils") works (the words are read in the same stemmed form `judge_materials` uses), a name that
  merges two things does not.
- A lesson moved or swapped in the timetable (`tt_move`, `tt_live`) puts its entry back into a line's `sources` without `alternatives` (`ADD_SOURCE` reads the view, which has no such column); the next
  build of the list, which the educator's page makes every time it opens, writes it again. A line read from `/materials/read` in between shows no "or".
- The sent checklist (`materials_text.checklist`) and the buy list's WhatsApp text say the one item, not "or crayons": only the class list on screen names the others.
- A person cannot make or break a group in the activity editor: it keeps what the fill marked. A note a person typed "A or B" into by hand is read by the fill only for an activity it has not split yet.
- A rebuild leaves a swapped line that the plan no longer asks for (like any line the educator changed) on the list; that is the existing rule for a changed line, unchanged here.
- Not looked at: a screen reader, a class list with several groups on one day, or alternatives with very long names at 400 px. Looked at by eye at 1440 and 400 px: the class list with an "or" line, its panel with
  the swap control, the activity page's materials with the "or" mark.
- `tests/test_materials_list.py::test_a_subject_educator_gets_only_her_subjects_materials` still fails on this database with and without this work.
