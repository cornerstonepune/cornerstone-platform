State on 17 Nov 2026, branch `cp-67-curriculum-tree`. Handed over at the context limit; the next session builds the screen.

## The bug, at its cause (read on the live database)
Grade 2 → Maths shows nothing because `app/(app)/setup/curriculum/flow.tsx` loads objectives only once a topic is chosen, and its
topics come only from `learning_objective.topic_code`, empty for every active objective (Grade 2 Maths: 122 objectives, 0 with a
topic, 24 units). The topic level is the objective's `unit` (2,591 of 2,593 active objectives have one).

## Done on this branch
- `apps/web/lib/curriculum-tree.ts`: `branches(grade)` gives every subject of a grade with its units and counts (objectives,
  distinct activities); `leaves(grade, subject, unit)` gives a unit's objectives. An objective with no subject or no unit sits under
  "No subject yet" / "No unit yet" (key `-`). Checked on live, read only: Grade 2's rows sum to 483 = its 483 active objectives.
