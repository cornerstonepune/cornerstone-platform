# CP-31: change a level's sentence from the engine (issue 625, part of 577)

`POST /curriculum/level-words` and `/curriculum/level-words/history` (`engine/api/routes/levels.py`, `engine/w1_bank/level_words.py`).
It is `saveWords`' level edit in the engine: `skill.difficulty -> level -> words` set, that level's approval withdrawn, who and
why kept by the database's own trigger (`history.by`, so `change_log` rows of `skill` and `skill_level`). No table, column,
function or migration. Tests: `tests/api/test_level_words_routes.py`.

## For the frontend
- Gate: the curriculum screens' (`curriculum_staff`): changing needs a review role (head, coordinator) or the educator switch;
  the preview only reads. History is a list of `{at, staff (id), was, now, reason}`; the site resolves the id to a name.
- Only the level's sentence: the skill's name and outcome stay on the old form until it is retired.
