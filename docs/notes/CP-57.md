# CP-57: Timetable in one place

Branch `cp-57-timetable-in-one-place`. Web only; no migration.

- `/timetable?view=fortnight` now carries what `/timetable/fortnight/[n]` and `/timetable/draft` did. The fortnight's own panel (state,
  next step, Go live, Conflicts / Moves / Draft / Rules) is the right side while no lesson is open; a lesson opened from the grid replaces
  it and its × brings it back. `app/(app)/timetable/fortnight-view.tsx` reads its data; `fortnight-panel.tsx` and `go-live.tsx` moved up
  from the old fortnight folder unchanged.
- Moving: the lesson panel's Move gives `from=day~period`; the grid then offers picks (not lesson opens) and the panel opens on Moves.
- The draft's grid is `show=draft` in the Fortnight view (coordinator). Nothing of the draft page's function was dropped.
- Old addresses answer 308 to the new ones, query kept (`redirects` in `next.config.ts`; the two old pages are deleted).
- Menu: the Fortnight line is gone; the timetable line is Week, whatever its view. `locate` no longer reads `?view=`.
- Children tab: `lessonView().markers` (class teacher and the subject's educator, today) named with a link to `/setup/staff?open=person:<id>`.
- Rejected: redirecting `/timetable/draft` with no grid replacement (it would lose the draft preview Nimish's staff use).
- Cards at the foot are not drawn in the Fortnight view (the card linked to itself).
