Other specs (m1-04) switch voice on and left it on. u90 now switches it on for its run and puts it back, as m1-04 does. Proved: that test passes alone on plain main's fresh database with the change. Tests only.

The coordinator's Today gets Papers to check and Notes to confirm between its tiles and "Waiting on you".
