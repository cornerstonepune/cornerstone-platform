# CP-53: the activity page on one screen, every part editable, machine links shown everywhere, a white site

Issue #773 (approved 14 Nov 2026). Branch `claude/gallant-bardeen-yi3uby`. Spec: `specs/CP-53/spec.md`.

## What changed
- **Engine.** `k2_review.change` takes any part, the school's included (Nimish, 14 Nov), and marks it the person's even when it had
  no mark (a sheet part); keep and reject still answer drafts only. `k2_review.set_materials` sets the materials as a list of items
  (a name in any case or spacing is one catalogue item; a new one waits for a home and a value, as `materials_list.propose_item`;
  a count under 1 refused); the text is written from the list. Route `POST /curriculum/page/materials`.
- **Activity page** (`components/activity-page.tsx`): the site's cards (`b-card` with its kind's colour), `RubricRows` from the
  lesson page with an "Edit Level n" each, the `Answers` editor on every part for a reviewer, a materials form of rows, "Approve all
  of this activity" (`ApproveForm` with words) as the main action while anything waits. The origin filter stays; `?tab=` is ignored.
- **Machine links everywhere.** The skill page's rail lists the skill's objectives and activities with "Linked by the machine · to
  approve" (`lib/origin.ts` `linkLine`). Its objective edges were never drawn on live: their screen's slice is not merged, so
  `screenHref` gave none and there was no fallback; each edge now carries its page as the fallback. Curriculum's Offers "Already
  linked" shows the machine's with its symbol and "to approve".
- **White site.** `ground` #fbfbfa (near-white, no warm cast: 13 Nov Nimish found pure white bleak), `menu` #f5f5f4.
- **Not changed:** Curriculum's side panel keeps its tabs and offers Edit on the machine's parts only (the spec is the activity page).
