# CP-84: one list of materials, with categories

## Engine (branch wt/catalogue, on wt/stock)

What was built, and where:

- `supabase/migrations/20270213090000_material_category.sql`: one column, `material_item.category` (nullable text, no constraint: the list is the
  setting `money.categories`, checked in the engine). No new table; `checks/rules.json` regenerated from a clean migrations-only database and did
  not change (a nullable column makes no rule).
- Settings (`supabase/seed/config.json`): `materials.category_words` (`seed_once`: a singular word and one of the school's categories; 30 words
  drafted from the 100 commonest words of the 1,423 activity notes, only the ones that always mean one category) and `materials.category_role`
  (`coordinator`: the lowest role on the money ladder that may set or change a category; a coordinator or a founder).
- `core/catalogue_key.py`: the one normaliser (lower case, one space, words split on anything that is not a letter or digit, apostrophes dropped,
  a plural read as its singular by a few rules and a short irregular list, each word once, in alphabetical order). "PENCIL pencils", "Pencils " and
  "pencil" are all the key `pencil`; "colour pencils" is `colour pencil`.
- `core/catalogue.py`: `make` (the one place an item is made: returns the item that already has the words, as its name or as a word a person
  approved, else makes one), `find`, `close` (items that share a word and are not it), `search`, `add`. `core/catalogue_category.py`: the category
  check, `categorise`, `set_category` (a coordinator or above; every change a `change_log` row, op `category`; the engine's own, origin
  `engine:categorise`).
- The three places that made an item now go through `make`: `materials_list.propose_item`, the catalogue fill's apply step
  (`w1_bank/map_ledger._material`, which also gives a new item the category its last word names) and an activity's materials written by a person
  (`w1_bank/k2_review._item`, which kept its own copy of the rule). `materials_review.approve_alias` refuses a word that is another item's name.
- `engine materials categorise [--dry-run]` (`w2_print/cli_materials.py`, on step N4 of `workflows.json`).
- Routes `api/routes/materials_items.py`, all POST with `actor`: `/materials/items/search`, `/add`, `/category`. `apps/web/lib/engine-routes.ts`
  regenerated.
- Tests: `tests/test_catalogue_key.py`, `tests/test_catalogue.py` (Done-when 1, 2, 3, 5 through the functions and the command),
  `tests/api/test_catalogue_routes.py` (Done-when 4 and 5 through the routes). None joined `tests/core.txt` (at its budget).

Decisions a reader should know:

- An item's category from its words is the category its LAST word names ("colour pencils" is a pencil, "chart paper" is paper); a name whose last
  word is not in the setting reads "needs a category". A wrong category costs a budget line, a missing one costs a click.
- Words in any order are one item, so "paper chart" and "chart paper" are one item. Asked for by Nimish's brief; two such items would have to be
  told apart by a person (a name in the other order is an approved word's job).
- `close` is the names that share a word with what was typed and are not it; it may repeat items already in `groups` (typing "pencils" finds
  "Colour pencils" in both), so a screen shows it when it wants a "did you mean".
- Adding a name that is already an item returns that item as it is (`created: false`); the category sent is not applied to it.
- Who may add: an educator who holds a class while the educator screens are on, or a coordinator or above whatever the switch says. Search is open
  to anyone on the staff list. Changing a category is for the role in `materials.category_role` and above.
- A category the school later drops from `money.categories` reads "needs a category" in the search; the stored word stays until someone sets another.

## The category rule reads the longest ending (found when the screens were reviewed)

The first rule took the last word only, so "glue stick" filed as TLM (stick) and "pencil box" as TLM (box). `catalogue_category.named` now tries the
longest ending of the name that the words list knows, then the last word alone. A row of `materials.category_words` may be a phrase ("glue stick",
"pencil box", "pencil case": stationery), kept as its words in the singular. Test: `test_the_longest_ending_of_a_name_that_the_words_know_decides…`.

## One thing written with and without a space (found on live data, 19 Nov; branch wt/merge, from main)

The K2 catalogue went live with 429 items, 8 of them pairs that were one thing: "Big bowl | Bigbowl", "black board | Blackboard", "Dust bin | Dustbin",
"flash card | flashcard", "Note Book | Notebook", "rubber band | rubberband", "Sketch pen | sketchpen", "T-Shirt | tshirt". The key compared a SET of
singular words, so two words never equalled one.

What was built, and where:

- `core/catalogue_key.py`: `squash(name)` (the singular words joined with no space, in the order written: "flash cards" is `flashcard`, "T-Shirt" is `tshirt`),
  `same_name(a, b)` (both have a word, and the key or the squash is equal; the word-set key stays, so the same words in any order are still one item) and
  `groups(named)` (the ids whose names are one thing, chained, by union-find over the two keys: linear, no two-by-two compare).
- `core/catalogue.py`: `_same`, `find`, `_close` (and so `make`, `add`, `materials_review.approve_alias` and the catalogue fill's `map_ledger._material`, which
  all go through them) compare through `same_name`, for an item's name and for its approved words. "colour pencils" and "pencils" are still two items, offered as close.
- `w2_print/materials_merge.py` and `engine materials merge [--dry-run]` (`w2_print/cli_materials.py`; `workflows.json` step N4: the file and the command). No new
  table, no migration. Per group: the keeper is the item the most activities use (`activity_material` rows), ties the lowest code. Every other item, in one
  transaction: its rows in EVERY table with a foreign key to `material_item` are pointed at the keeper (the tables, their unique keys and whether they are append-only
  are read from `pg_constraint`, `pg_index` and `pg_trigger`, so a table added later is moved too; one test makes such a table and proves it); a row the keeper already has the
  like of is dropped first (an activity that has both; a list with a line of each); its name and approved words become approved words of the keeper
  (`materials_text.norm`, the form `approve_alias` stores); a blank category, home or value of the keeper is filled from it (nothing the keeper has is overwritten;
  conversions: the keeper's win); one `change_log` row (row: the keeper, op `merge`, origin `engine:merge`, before: the item as it was, after: `into`, the keeper's
  approved words, rows moved and dropped per table); the item is deleted. An item with a row in a table kept for ever (`stock_move`: its trigger is `forbid_change`) is not
  merged: it is said ("has stock recorded; merge it by hand") and left. A dry run does the same work inside a savepoint and rolls it back, so its counts are the real run's
  and it changes nothing. A second run finds nothing (items left for stock are said again).
- `.github/curriculum/command.py` and `curriculum-run.yml`: two fixed runs, `materials_merge_preview` (`engine materials merge --dry-run`) and `materials_merge` (`engine materials merge`);
  neither spends; the choice list is RUNS in order.
- Tests: `tests/test_catalogue_key.py` (squash, same_name for the 8 real pairs, groups), `tests/test_catalogue.py` (the 8 pairs are one item through `find`/`make`/`add` in both orders,
  close stays close, an approved word is compared the same way), `tests/test_catalogue_merge.py` (tables read from the database; a dry run changes nothing; a real run with the
  clash cases, the history row and the aliases; the keeper rule; the 8 pairs; stock left alone; the command), `tests/test_curriculum_run_command.py`. None joined `tests/core.txt`.
