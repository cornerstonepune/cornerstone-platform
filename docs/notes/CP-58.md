# CP-58: Time by subject, planned and taught

Branch `cp-58-time-by-subject` (built on `cp-57-…` until #790 merges). No migration.

- Engine: `/timetable/time` rows gain `done_lessons` and `done_minutes` (sessions whose state is `done`, each its period's length) and the
  range gains `done_minutes`; `minutes` is untouched (it is the timetable's planned time). New `everyone: true` asks for every class with
  lessons in the range (coordinators; an educator is refused 403). Tests in `tests/test_tt_time.py`.
- Web: `lib/time-by-subject.ts` `periodOf` (week = Monday to Sunday, month and quarter by the calendar, term from config `school.terms`
  `[{label, starts_on, ends_on}]`, else custom `from`/`to`). `This term` is not offered until the school names its terms: no dates are
  invented. Open question for Nimish: the term dates, and whether "quarter" is the calendar quarter.
- The week columns and the ring are gone from the page (their weekly view was the "more classes/lessons" he did not want); the table with
  planned and taught bars is the page. A subject's row opens its lessons of the period on the right.
- Materials: its menu line is gone; `/timetable/materials` stays; the lesson panel's Materials tab links to it for that class and day.
