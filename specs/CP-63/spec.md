# CP-63 (#796): Today in blocks

### What
Nimish, 16 Nov 2026: "Today is a line-item list; it should be different sections. One section where I know exactly what I teach today: a small timetable snapshot; I click and the timetable opens for that day showing all periods; I click an activity and can put in the rubric. For assessments, two or three things: if I have to check papers, cards ..."
Today shows blocks, each under its own heading: Teach today (the day's periods: time, subject, activity name or "no activity yet"; each opens that lesson in the timetable's own panel for that day); Papers to check (one small card per assessment waiting: title, "n answers to judge", one button to its check page, and "See all assessments"; no second board); Notes to confirm; and, under its own heading, what else Today lists. Every block says in words when it is empty. Educators read their own classes; a coordinator reads the school's.

### Done when
1. Today shows the blocks in this order: Teach today, Papers to check, Notes to confirm, what else waits.
2. A click on a period opens that lesson in the timetable's panel for that day; "no activity yet" is said for a period with none; the block links to the timetable for the day.
3. Each paper waiting is one small card (title, answers to judge, one button) whose button opens its check page; "See all assessments" opens the board.
4. A block with nothing in it says so in words.

### Out of scope
A second Kanban board. Rebuilding the lesson panel or the rubric (the timetable's lesson panel is reused). Database changes. Names of children.
