Part of #577 / UI-3c (the new skill screen's "change a level's sentence"). Does the edit `saveWords` does, in the engine, so it is logged with who and when, and returns the level's history. No table, column, function or migration: `skill` already keeps the history through its trigger (`change_log`, tables `skill` and `skill_level`).

## What
Two engine routes, for the curriculum screens (same gate as the other curriculum routes: the person is on the staff list and holds a review role; the educator preview only looks):
- `POST /curriculum/level-words` {actor, skill, level, words, reason?}: changes one level's sentence on `skill.difficulty`, withdraws that level's approval (so whoever approves next has read these words), and keeps who and why in `change_log`.
- `POST /curriculum/level-words/history` {actor, skill, level}: the level's sentence now, whether it is approved, and each earlier sentence with when and which member of staff (a staff id, never a name).

## Done when
1. A stranger is refused (403); a person without a review role cannot change a sentence (403) but can read the history; nothing is written on a refusal.
2. A new sentence replaces only that level's words: the other levels, the level's rule and the skill's other fields are unchanged.
3. That level's approval is withdrawn and no other level's is.
4. `change_log` gets the skill's and the level's rows with the person's staff id and the reason; the history route lists the earlier sentence, the new one, when and who, newest first.
5. The same sentence sent again changes nothing: no approval withdrawn, no log row, `changed: false`.
6. Refused with the reason, nothing written: an empty sentence, one over 400 characters, an unknown skill or level (404/422).

## Out of scope
Editing a skill's name or outcome (the old page's form stays until retired), approving a level, the frontend, a new table.
