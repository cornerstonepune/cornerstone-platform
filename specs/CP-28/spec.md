Part of #595. Gives the website the school's counts for the coordinator's Today (wireframe `today-coordinator`), which it cannot read today. Read-only: no table, no function, no schema change.

## What
One engine route, `POST /k2/today-school` (body: the actor, like `/k2/coordinator`), coordinator only (the same check as `/k2/coordinator`; others get 403). It answers whole numbers and codes only: no name of a child or educator, no rate, no score, no ordering of any person.

## Done when
1. A person without a coordinator role is refused (403); a coordinator of another campus sees only their own campus.
2. `clashes`: clashes nobody accepted, in fortnights not yet ended, equal to the sum of `tt_clash.open_by_kind`.
3. `drafts`: classes whose newest placed draft for a fortnight that has not started has no `timetable_live` approval; one count and one row per fortnight number and class (grade, section).
4. `late_notes` equals `/k2/coordinator` counts `late` (one source, not a second rule).
5. `materials_no_quantity`: live lines (not removed) with no quantity, needed next week, by grade.
6. `last_week`: one cell per class per teaching day, Monday to Friday of last week: `done`, `moved`, `not_done` or `holiday` (holiday if the day is a holiday; `not_done` if any planned lesson that day is not done or still unconfirmed; else `moved` if any moved; else `done`), and `sessions_done` / `sessions_planned` totals where moved counts as taught.
7. `consent`: children enrolled now with consent given and not later withdrawn, of children enrolled now.
8. `activities_ready`: activities with steps and all three levels, of all; `materials_ready`: activity materials with a quantity, of all.
9. `new_next_week`: activities in next week's lessons that no lesson before them has used, with their subject and the classes (grade, section) they fall in.
10. Every count comes from rows by arithmetic in SQL; no model; the route writes nothing (a test counts rows before and after).
