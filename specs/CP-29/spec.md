## What
One engine route, `POST /timetable/go-live` (engine key, the person's email as `actor`). Body: grade, section, year, fortnight number, optional draft id (default: the newest placed draft of that class and fortnight), optional reason, `discard_changes`, `dry`. The rules are CP-22's, unchanged: only the school's go-live group, the fortnight not yet started, the draft placed and current, nothing changed by hand since it last went live unless the person says to discard it. The answer is the report in whole numbers and codes: no name.

## Done when
1. A person not on the staff list, or not in the go-live group, is refused (403 / 409 with CP-22's words) and nothing is written.
2. `dry: true` answers the report and writes nothing (a test counts approvals and moved lessons before and after).
3. A real go-live updates the fortnight's sessions in place (same ids, plan lines kept) and writes one `timetable_live` approval row naming who, which draft and whether changes were discarded.
4. The answer lists every session it moved (id, subject, from date, to date and period) and every lesson it removed, and how many it added, and nothing else; the day-by-day counts equal CP-22's `lines()`.
5. Each CP-22 refusal (fortnight started, frozen with no reason, changed since it went live, draft not placed or stale, no such class or fortnight) answers 409 with the reason and writes nothing.
6. With no draft id given, the newest placed draft is used; none placed answers 409 "no placed draft".

## Out of scope
The frontend, a new table, changing CP-22's rules, going live for a whole school in one call.
