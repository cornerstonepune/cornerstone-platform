# CP-66: top navigation bar

Issue #824. Approved by Nimish in chat on 17 Nov 2026 ("should we move on the top side so that the whole page becomes available").

## What
- The left side menu is replaced by one top bar: logo, Today, Timetable, Children, Assessments, Curriculum, then search and the user menu.
- Setup (Staff, Classes, Timetable rules, How it works) moves inside the user menu.
- Pages use the full width.
- The bar is white with one accent colour for the active item, and badges are quiet.
- On a phone, a bottom tab bar holds at most 5 items.

## Done when
1. At 1440 px there is no side menu, and the top bar shows those links in that order.
2. Setup's four links are reachable from the user menu.
3. At 400 px a bottom tab bar has at most 5 items, and the page has no horizontal scroll.
4. The active page is marked in the bar.
5. u90, the design-system test, passes.

## Out of scope
Any change to page content.
