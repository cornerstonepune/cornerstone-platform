## What
`GET /capture/{id}/pages` (`same_page.pages_of`) decodes and hashes every full-size page photograph on every call: 0.40 to 0.48 s a call (frontend builder measurement, unverified by me). The paper screen calls it on every open, and `p6-check-is-quick` "the paper screen opens on a paper of 7 answers waiting within its budget" measured about 2.8 s against 2.5 s, which keeps main red and holds back every live change. Keep a page fingerprint while its file is unchanged.

3. `p6` passes on main CI (judged on the CI run, not locally).
